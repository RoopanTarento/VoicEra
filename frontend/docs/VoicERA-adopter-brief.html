Five ready-made applications

  • Scheme Discovery · citizen says a need, hears which schemes may apply and how to apply. In Platinum.
  • Citizen Surveys · outbound feedback calls, answers captured as a table. In Platinum.
  • Basic Form Filling · collects fields by voice, then needs a form or API.
  • Scheme Application Support · documents, missing items, status. Needs your system.
  • Citizen Grievance · file, route, track, close. Needs your ticketing system.

How adoption works

From a sentence to a live service

Nine steps. Most of them are yours, and the setup clock only starts when you have handed everything over.

Figure 2. The adoption journey. Blue steps are done by VoicERA package support. Steps 1 to 3 can be done in the Studio and the proof-of-concept builder.

What you bring

  • A use case and what success looks like
  • A conversation script with intro, questions, closing
  • A basic knowledge base: up to 10 text files, 5 MB each
  • Citizen phone numbers for outbound, with permission to call
  • People to test calls after setup, before launch
  • Approvals and a named owner on your side

What package support does

  • Sets up the voice agent in 2 working days once inputs are in
  • Provides telephony and numbers, at ₹500 each
  • Runs campaign set-up and monitoring: 1 operator, 1 shared programme manager
  • Troubleshoots within 24 hours
  • Shares analysis within 3 working days of a campaign
  • Hosts and bills through Bhashini

What usually holds a launch up

  • Script not approved when inputs are handed over
  • Knowledge over the limit: more than 10 files, or over 5 MB
  • Phone list or permission to call not ready
  • A system link discovered late. It is out of scope and needs its own assessment
  • Data questions unanswered: storage, training, retention
  • Nobody named to test calls, approve, or own the results

Try it before you commit: three tools in the experience

  • VoicERA Studio: say what you want. It draws the call, flags what is outside the package, shows what would make it fit, and plays the conversation.
  • Package advisor: asks only the questions that matter, then gives a mapped recommendation, checklist, plan and cost.
  • Scheme Discovery proof of concept: enter your schemes once and get a script, ready knowledge files inside the limits, and a launch checklist.
